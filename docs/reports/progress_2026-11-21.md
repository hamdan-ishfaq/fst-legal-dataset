## Measured (base Qwen3-8B, details in `rlm_vs_baseline.md`)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gold provision recall | 0.269 | 0.098 | 0.171 | 0.106 |
| Briefs produced | 28/28 | 27/28 | 13/14 | 14/14 |
| Claims removed | 14% | 32% | 21.6% | 22.3% |
| Time per brief | 42 s | 176 s | 47 s | 150 s |
| Sub-calls per run | – | 0.8 | – | 0 |

On the base model the RLM loses on recall and time; it barely delegates. Long input narrows the removal-rate gap and the RLM never runs out of window.
